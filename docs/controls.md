# Controls

[Back to the README](../README.md)

Every control works with the buttons and the touch screen. KaiRadio is drawn
on the GPU, sharp in handheld and docked.

## Buttons

| Button | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **A** | Play the selected station (in World: open the country; in Now Playing: play or stop) |
| **Y** | In World: a random station. Elsewhere: add to favorites, or remove; on a list of genres, countries or languages, sort it (most or fewest stations, A to Z, Z to A) |
| **X** | Now Playing; in Search by name, type a name |
| **ZL** / **ZR** | Previous / next tab |
| **L** / **R** | Previous / next station, in the list you played from |
| **◀** / **▶** | In World: the country that way. In Search: by name, genre, country or language. Elsewhere: a page up or down |
| **↑** / **↓** | Move in the list; in World, the country that way |
| **B** | Back (out of Now Playing, or out of a genre, country or language, to the map if opened from it) |
| **−** | Play or stop |
| **+** | Menu: Settings, or exit (press **+** again to exit) |

## Touch

Tap:

- a station to play it, and its heart to keep it;
- a tab, genre or country to open it;
- a country on the globe to pick it (tap it again, or Open its stations, to
  open it);
- Random station beside the globe;
- the sort at the top right of a list of genres, countries or languages to
  change it;
- the bar at the bottom for Now Playing;
- the title at the top left to open the menu, and the **×** before it to
  exit.

Drag the globe to turn it, and a list to scroll it: a quick flick keeps it
gliding, and a touch stops it.
