# Finding stations

[Back to the README](../README.md)

The stations come from the community
[Radio Browser](https://www.radio-browser.info/) directory: tens of thousands
of public stations.

## World

- A night globe, each country lit up by how many stations it has.
- Turn it with a finger, or go from country to country with either stick.
- It opens on the country of the station playing.
- **Tap a country** and one of its stations plays, picked at random; tap it
  again for another one.
- Beside it, the country picked: its flag, how many stations it has, and its
  **time and weather** right now.
- Open it (**A**, or its card) to see all its stations.
- The globe can be a flat night map instead (Settings > World view).

## Random station

**Y** in World: a country at random, the globe turned to it, and one of its
stations playing. **L** and **R** go through the rest.

## Popular and search

- **Popular**: the stations played most, worldwide.
- **Search** by **name** (with the console's keyboard), or browse:
  - **genres** (each with an icon),
  - **countries** (with their flags),
  - **languages** (with letters in their own script),

  each with how many stations it has.

## Favorites and Recent

**Favorites** and **Recent** are kept on your card, in KaiRadio's folder (see
[Where KaiRadio keeps its files](settings.md#where-kairadio-keeps-its-files)).
